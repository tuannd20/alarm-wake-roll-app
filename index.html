Is Alarm Wake Roll free?

The app can be downloaded for free and may show ads. Optional paid features, if offered, are handled by Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no registration and we don't ask for your name, phone number or email.

Why doesn't my alarm ring on time?

Some phones restrict background apps. Allow notifications and exact alarms for Alarm Wake Roll and exclude it from battery optimization in your device settings.
