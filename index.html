FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Alarm Wake Roll free?

The app can be downloaded for free from the App Store and may show ads. Optional paid features, if offered, are billed through your Apple ID.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no registration and we don't ask for your name, phone number or email.

Why doesn't my alarm ring on time?

Make sure notifications and sounds are allowed for Alarm Wake Roll in iPhone Settings, check that your volume is up and Focus / Do Not Disturb isn't silencing it, and avoid force-closing the app from the app switcher.
