Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Alarm Wake Roll app ("App") accesses, uses and protects information. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

  • Your alarms and settings stay on your device.
  • No account is required; we do not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Third-party SDKs (such as advertising and analytics) may collect device information.

2. Information We Access and Collect

2.1 On-device data

Alarm times, labels, repeat days, sound, vibration and snooze preferences, and app settings such as theme or language are stored locally on your device.

2.2 Automatically collected by third parties

Third-party services integrated in the App may automatically collect the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and app version, language, country, crash logs, diagnostics and usage data.

3. How We Use Information

  • to operate the App and ring your alarms;
  • to maintain, secure and improve the App;
  • to show and measure advertisements;
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. Permissions

The App may request: notifications, exact alarm scheduling, vibration, running at device startup so alarms survive a reboot, audio/media access for custom sounds, and network access. You can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use Google Play services, Google AdMob and its ad partners, and analytics or crash-reporting tools such as Firebase. These providers have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising technologies
  • Firebase privacy information

6. Advertising

The App may show personalized or non-personalized ads. The advertising ID may be used for this purpose. You can reset it or opt out of ads personalization in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Where required, such as in the EEA and UK, we request your consent first.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared only with service providers that help us operate the App, when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and security.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Your alarms and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Retention by third-party providers follows their own policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please keep your device protected.

10. Your Rights and Choices

You can control permissions, delete your alarms, and reset or opt out of the advertising ID. Depending on your region (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data. Because the App has no user accounts, we generally hold no directly identifying personal data. To exercise your rights, contact us below.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International Transfers

Third-party providers may process data in countries other than your own, with appropriate safeguards where required.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
